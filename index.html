My movies The movies you own in the game, read from your savegame. Only those get scheduled, each one once. Fresher movies win (freshness is read from your savegame): Mid ones count a bit less and Low ones much less, so a fresh movie of another genre can take their slot. Between equal movies the higher rating (0 to 10) airs first. Tags change how each audience takes a movie: TV show +25% per episode already aired, Cult +10%, +16 (children avoid it), XXX (only from 23.00), Glamour, Cartoon and Anime.

Make your own movie: pick a genre and type the points you have. Every level costs as many points as its number, so a 3 costs 1 + 2 + 3 = 6 and a full 10 costs 55. At 180, 280, 355 and 375 points you get the best mix from the guide; any other amount starts from the guide mix just below it and is adjusted to fit your points.
